VeriHome Compliance Standard

1. Introduction & Commitment to Privacy

At VeriHome Inspection Services LLC ("VeriHome", "we", "us", or "our"), we place the highest priority on safeguarding the confidentiality of our clients, homebuyers, real estate agents, and property sellers. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of personal, contact, and structural diagnostic data we collect through our website, mobile portals, and on-site inspection activities, and how we protect and govern that data.

2. Information We Collect

To provide certified home inspections, structural audits, and environmental laboratory evaluations, we collect:

  • Client Contact Details: Full name, email address, telephone numbers, and billing details.
  • Property Inspection Data: Target property street address, square footage, building age, lockbox/access codes, and current occupancy status.
  • Diagnostic Media & Findings: High-resolution digital photographs, 4K aerial drone roof recordings, FLIR® infrared thermograms, and lab test specimen records.
  • Account Credentials: Username, encrypted passwords, and session identifiers for the VeriHome Customer Dashboard and Client Portal.

3. Strict Report Confidentiality

Confidentiality Guarantee: Your completed property inspection report is your exclusive, proprietary property. VeriHome will never provide, sell, or disclose your report or diagnostic photos to the property seller, listing broker, mortgage lender, or municipal inspectors without your express written authorization.

4. How We Use Collected Data

VeriHome uses collected information exclusively for lawful business and diagnostic operations:

  • Conducting comprehensive 400-point on-site inspections in compliance with InterNACHI standards.
  • Publishing and delivering interactive 24-hour cloud-hosted digital inspection reports.
  • Transmitting environmental air and water specimens to accredited third-party laboratories (e.g., Radon CRM and Mold spore analysis).
  • Administering the $10,000 InterNACHI "We'll Buy Your Home Back" guarantee registration.
  • Sending booking confirmations, inspector arrival SMS notifications, and invoice receipts.

5. Data Storage, Security & Encryption

We employ bank-grade 256-bit TLS/SSL encryption for all data in transit across our portal and cloud storage. Inspection media and digital reports are hosted on SOC-2 certified cloud infrastructure with strict role-based access control. Inspector field tablets require multi-factor biometric authentication to access scheduling and client data.

6. Cookies & Browser Analytics

Our website utilizes essential session cookies to remember your preferred UI theme (Dark/Light mode), RTL text alignment, and secure login session. We do not engage in invasive cross-site advertising networks or data brokerage.

7. Your Rights & Data Requests

You retain the right to review, update, download, or request permanent deletion of your customer profile and historical inspection archives at any time. To make a data access or deletion request, please contact our Data Governance Officer at privacy@verihome.com.
