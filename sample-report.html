MAJOR DEFECT Section 2.1 - Roof Coverings
Requires Immediate Action
Cracked Shingles & Damaged Flashing Around Chimney

Finding: Asphalt architectural shingles adjacent to the chimney flashing on the south slope exhibit broken seal tabs and cracked flashing sealant. With an inspector ruler gauge, approximately 1.5 inches of shingle uplift was recorded. Active signs of moisture intrusion were noted on the roof deck underlayment.

Inspector Recommendation: Engage a licensed roofing contractor to replace affected shingles and reinstall galvanized step flashing before closing. Estimated repair cost: $800 - $1,400.
Photo 2.1A: 1.5" shingle gap measured
SAFETY HAZARD Section 5.3 - Service Panel
Fire & Shock Hazard
Double-Tapped Circuit Breaker in Kitchen Branch Line

Finding: Inside the main 200A Siemens electrical breaker panel, breaker slot A16 (Kitchen Outlets) has two conductors attached under a single terminal lug that is only rated for one wire. Klein Tools digital multimeter recorded resistance variance and potential arcing risk under high load.

Inspector Recommendation: A licensed master electrician must properly pigtail the wires or install an approved tandem/piggyback breaker. Estimated repair cost: $150 - $250.
Photo 5.3B: Attention Required tag on slot A16
SAFETY HAZARD Section 6.4 - Water Heating Equipment
Scald / Pressure Hazard
Water Heater TPR Relief Valve Missing Discharge Tube

Finding: The 50-gallon gas water heater in the basement has an operational Temperature & Pressure Relief (TPR) valve, but the discharge port lacks an extension pipe directed downward to within 6 inches of the floor drain. If excess pressure builds, scalding hot steam could project outward horizontally.

Inspector Recommendation: Install a rigid 3/4-inch copper or CPVC discharge pipe extending downward to prevent scalding. Estimated repair cost: $60 - $120.
Photo 6.4A: Unpiped TPR relief discharge valve
MINOR DEFECT Section 7.2 - Cooling Equipment
Maintenance Needed
AC Exterior Condenser Coil Fins Bent & Restricted Airflow

Finding: The outdoor condensing unit has roughly 15% crushed aluminum coil fins along the lower perimeter, likely caused by weed trimmers. System operated normally with a 18°F temperature differential, but reduced airflow lowers seasonal energy efficiency.

Inspector Recommendation: Comb coil fins straight using an HVAC fin comb tool during routine spring service. Estimated cost: $80 - $150.
Photo 7.2A: Bent aluminum condenser coil fins
MINOR DEFECT Section 6.2 - Fixtures & Drain Waste Vent
Minor Seepage
Under-Sink PVC P-Trap Slip-Nut Seepage

Finding: Moisture testing beneath the primary bathroom vanity revealed active dripping along the slip-joint nut of the 1-1/2" PVC P-trap during continuous drainage testing. Rubber gasket has dry-rotted. Minor wood swelling noted on vanity cabinet base.

Inspector Recommendation: Replace rubber slip-joint beveled washer and hand-tighten PVC compression nut. Estimated repair cost: $40 - $75.
Photo 6.2B: Active water droplet on PVC trap
SATISFACTORY Section 1.1 - Foundation & Framing
Structurally Sound
Poured Concrete Foundation & Main Steel Support I-Beam

Finding: Foundation walls, crawlspace stem walls, and steel support posts were inspected and verified with a Zip-Level digital altimeter. Minor hairline curing cracks (< 1/16") were observed, which are typical for concrete hydration. No settlement displacement or water efflorescence was found.

Inspector Status: Structure and load-bearing framing meet InterNACHI standards in good serviceable condition. No structural repairs needed.
Photo 1.1A: Plumb poured concrete foundation wall
SATISFACTORY Section 5.1 - Main Service Equipment
Fully Compliant
200-Amp Main Service Disconnect & Grounding Electrode System

Finding: Main service copper conductors, 200A main breaker disconnect, neutral/ground bonding bus, and dual driven ground rods were inspected. Dead front panel was removed; thermal imaging confirmed normal baseline operating temperatures under full simulated load.

Inspector Status: Clean, code-compliant residential electrical distribution panel with proper wire gauges and clear labeling.
