Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about scheduling your inspection, report turnaround times, pricing, and our licensed inspectors.

We maintain multiple certified inspectors on active rotation Monday through Saturday, allowing us to schedule most appointments within 24 to 48 hours. If you are under a tight real estate contingency deadline (such as a 3-day contingency), please indicate this in the contact form or call our direct dispatch line at (800) 555-HOME for priority same-day scheduling.

We guarantee full digital report delivery within 24 hours of inspection completion, and often by 8:00 PM the very same evening. You will receive an SMS and email with a secure link to access your interactive online report, high-resolution photo gallery, infrared thermal captures, and 1-click PDF download.

Yes, absolutely! While you don't need to stay for the entire 3 to 4 hours, we strongly recommend attending the final 45 to 60 minutes for a personal walkthrough. The inspector will walk you through the property, show you main emergency shutoffs (water, gas, electrical), review all major and minor findings, and answer any questions you have on the spot.

We cover the entire metropolitan area and surrounding communities within a 50-mile radius of our central offices. This includes suburban and urban properties across all major regional counties. You can view our live coverage area on the interactive OpenStreetMap above. For commercial or specialized projects outside this zone, contact our dispatch team for tailored travel arrangements.

Our comprehensive inspection evaluates over 400 checkpoint items adhering to strict InterNACHI Standards of Practice. This includes the roof and flashing, foundation and crawlspace, exterior grading and drainage, HVAC systems, electrical panels and wiring, plumbing lines and water heaters, attic insulation and ventilation, windows, doors, and built-in appliances.

Yes! Our digital reporting portal includes an integrated Repair Request Builder. You or your real estate agent can select specific defect items from the report, attach contractor repair estimates, and automatically generate a formal PDF repair addendum to submit to the seller.

We understand that transactions and closing timelines can shift unexpectedly. You may reschedule or cancel your appointment at no charge with at least 24 hours notice. In the event of severe weather (heavy snow, freezing rain, or roof safety hazards), we will complete the interior inspection as scheduled and return to complete the exterior roof audit at no additional charge as soon as conditions clear.

Every VeriHome inspector is an InterNACHI-certified professional, state-licensed (State License #HI-89420), thoroughly background-checked, and backed by comprehensive $1,000,000 Errors & Omissions (E&O) and General Liability insurance policies.
