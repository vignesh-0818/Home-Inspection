Diagnostic Tools That See What the Human Eye Misses

We invest in commercial-grade diagnostic hardware to provide empirical proof, not guesswork. This empirical data gives you unbeatable leverage during price negotiations.

FLIR® Infrared Cam

Detects hidden plumbing leaks, missing insulation, and overheating electrical breakers.

4K Aerial Drone

Reveals broken clay tiles, loose flashing, and chimney cracks that cannot be seen safely from ladders.

Pinless Moisture Meters

Quantifies moisture percentage behind tile walls and hardwood floors without drilling holes.

Zip-Level Foundation Alt

Measures foundation floor elevation differences across the property to detect settling.
