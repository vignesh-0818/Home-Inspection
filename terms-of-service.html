2. Standards of Practice & Diagnostic Protocol

All inspections performed by VeriHome are visual, non-invasive examinations of the readily accessible installed systems and components of the designated real property. Our inspections conform strictly to the Standards of Practice established by the International Association of Certified Home Inspectors (InterNACHI) and state licensing boards.

Visual Inspection Scope: An inspection is not technically exhaustive. The inspector will not dismantle equipment, open sealed walls, excavate soil, or endanger personal safety to access unsafe structural zones.

3. Limitations & Exclusions

The following items and conditions are outside the scope of a standard property assessment unless specifically ordered as an add-on service:

  • Concealed plumbing within slabs, underground sewer main lines (requires Sewer Scope Camera Add-on).
  • Indoor airborne mold spore concentration and toxicity (requires Mold Spore Lab Test Add-on).
  • Subsurface radioactive radon gas concentration (requires Radon 48-Hour CRM Add-on).
  • Swimming pools, hot tubs, spas, and automated chlorination systems (requires Pool & Spa Add-on).
  • Latent defects, intermittent electrical anomalies, or environmental conditions concealed by furniture, wall coverings, or deliberate seller staging.

4. Client Attendance & Walkthrough Review

We strongly encourage the Client and their real estate representative to attend the concluding 45 minutes of the inspection for the on-site forensic walkthrough. This enables the certified inspector to demonstrate primary shutoff valves, explain defect severity ratings, and answer specific technical questions in person.

5. Report Delivery & Intellectual Property

Upon payment in full, VeriHome delivers the interactive digital inspection report via secure cloud link within 24 hours of inspection completion. The report, high-resolution photographs, 4K aerial recordings, and thermal scans are provided for the exclusive use of the named Client in evaluating the target property.

6. InterNACHI $10,000 Buy-Back Guarantee

Qualifying single-family residential pre-purchase inspections include InterNACHI's "We'll Buy Your Home Back" guarantee. Terms, conditions, and filing deadlines are governed exclusively by InterNACHI's official guarantee program guidelines available at nachi.org/buy.

Appointments may be rescheduled or cancelled without penalty with at least 24 hours prior written notice. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours notice or instances where the inspector is unable to gain property access may be subject to a $75 trip and administrative fee.
